A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Raised in the Upstate, I help families find where they truly belong.

For nearly a decade I’ve had the privilege of guiding people through one of the most meaningful decisions of their lives — finding home. Whether you’re moving to Greenville for the first time, upgrading for your growing family, or selling the house that holds decades of memories, I bring local knowledge, calm guidance, and a genuine commitment to your success.
