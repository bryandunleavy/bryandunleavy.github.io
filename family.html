THE MOST IMPORTANT THING

Family is my
why.

My wife Emily and I have built a life and family here in the Upstate that we are incredibly grateful for. Everything else — my career, my volunteer work, the way I treat clients — flows from this foundation.

Emily & Me

Emily is my best friend, my biggest supporter, and the person who keeps me grounded. Whether we’re hiking the Blue Ridge trails, exploring new restaurants in downtown Greenville, or just enjoying a quiet evening at home, our time together is sacred.

We love this region — the four seasons, the welcoming communities, the way the mountains meet the foothills. It’s the kind of place that makes you want to slow down and savor life.
