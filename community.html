GIVING BACK TO THE PLACE I LOVE

Community
is everything.

I’ve been blessed to build a life and career here in the Upstate. That comes with a responsibility to give back. My volunteer work isn’t something I do on the side — it’s core to who I am.

IRISH HERITAGE
Greenville St. Patrick’s Day Parade & Festival
I proudly serve as Vice President and Executive Board Member of the Greenville St. Patrick’s Day Parade & Festival committee. Every March we bring together thousands of people to celebrate Irish culture, heritage, and the incredible spirit of our community.
One of the largest St. Patrick’s celebrations in the Southeast
FRATERNAL ORDER
Ancient Order of Hibernians
I serve as President of the Harp & Shamrock Division of the Ancient Order of Hibernians. The AOH is the oldest Catholic fraternal organization in the United States, dedicated to friendship, unity, and true Christian charity.
Preserving Irish culture and supporting our local community
CHILDHOOD CANCER RESEARCH
St. Baldrick’s Foundation
For several years I have served as a team captain and fundraiser for St. Baldrick’s, shaving my head on live television to raise money and awareness for childhood cancer research. It is one of the most meaningful things I do each year.
100% of donations go directly to research
WHY I DO THIS WORK

I believe we have a duty to leave our community better than we found it.

The same people who buy homes from me, who wave at me on Main Street, and who celebrate Irish heritage with me every March — those are the people I’m here to serve.

Whether it’s raising money for kids fighting cancer or putting on a parade that brings the whole city together, I show up because I care deeply about this place and these people.
